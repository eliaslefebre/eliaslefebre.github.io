Education

I graduated cum laude in 2024 with a bachelor's degree in Game Development (specialization in Game Sound Integration) from Digital Arts & Entertainment.

I started in Independent Game Development because, in high school, I was fascinated by how video games combine art and programming to let people experience beautiful stories. During a group project in my 4th semester I discovered a love for audio, and pivoted to Game Sound Integration.
